ABOUT THE HERITAGE

Rock-Hewn Churches of Lalibela

Eleven churches carved from solid rock in the 12th–13th century under King Lalibela. A UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Our Lady Mary of Zion, Axum

The spiritual centre of the church in the ancient capital of Axum. Tradition holds that the Ark of the Covenant rests here.

Debre Damo Monastery

Founded in the 6th century by Abuna Aregawi, one of the Nine Saints. Monks reach the cliff-top site by climbing a rope.

Yeha and Abuna Aftse

Yeha holds a temple about 2,700 years old, built before Christianity arrived. A monastery named for Abuna Aftse stands beside it.

Abuna Yemata Guh

A cliff church in the Gheralta mountains of Tigray, linked to Abuna Yemata, another of the Nine Saints. Its painted interior is reached by a steep climb.

Island and peninsula monasteries dating from the 14th century, known for their richly painted walls.

Debre Berhan Selassie, Gondar

Built in the 17th century under Emperor Iyasu I. Its ceiling is covered with painted angel faces.

Debre Libanos

Founded in the 13th century by Abune Tekle Haymanot, one of the most revered saints of Ethiopia, north of Addis Ababa.

Gishen Mariam

A mountain monastery in Wollo. Tradition holds that it keeps a fragment of the True Cross.

Built in the late 1800s under Emperor Menelik II, on the hills where the capital's story began.
